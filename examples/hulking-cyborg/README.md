# Hulking cyborg

Official reference: [six-view sheet](../../showcase/reference-cyborg/image-01.png).
The reference was generated with OpenAI image generation and selected by the owner.

The muscular human torso, left arm and right leg contrast with the slimmer artificial
right arm and left leg. Artificial surfaces are broad black carbon-fibre panels and
ivory ceramic, without chrome. The white android faceplate uses luminous eye and mouth graphics.
Left and right always mean the character's own sides.

The modeling contract has a 60,000-triangle ceiling. The chosen working height is 2.6 m;
the concept drawing does not prescribe physical scale. Its views are approximate raster
projections, not calibrated renders of one 3D model.

The motion contract requests idle, walk, run, jump, punch and angry_face.
Walk and run loop in place. Face animation changes the luminous graphics.
