# Six-limbed horror: approved v8 frozen comparison brief

Build the creature in sheet.png, unchanged approved v8. Blender metres, +Z up,
+Y toward the face, anatomical left X<0. Centre the overall bounding box at the
origin. Approximate ground Z=-0.775 m. No floor, text, backdrop or external image.

- Elongated horizontal gaunt humanlike torso, with visible ribs, a narrow waist,
  sinewy shoulders and pelvis. One bald humanoid head projects forward on a long
  lean neck; deep eyes, nose, ears, tight mouth and angular cheek/jaw silhouette.
  No tail, wings, horns, extra heads, clothes or props are visible.
- Exactly six jointed supporting limbs in three bilateral pairs, attached at
  anterior, middle and posterior trunk stations. Long tapered humanlike segments,
  enlarged angular joints, slender wrists/ankles and splayed humanlike feet/toes.
  Keep six roots and six terminals traceable in 3D even when occluded in a view.
  Do not convert them to insect exoskeleton, extra arms or eight spider legs.
- Pale warm skin: tan midtones, lighter bone/skin ridges, brown recesses and muted
  reddish patches. Use the common frozen palette. Ribs and tendons are anatomical
  relief rather than painted black stripes. Keep detail subordinate to silhouette.
- FRONT governs head/front proportions and lateral leg spread. LEFT governs
  total depth, horizontal torso, head projection and three fore/aft attachment
  stations. TOP confirms three paired roots and six terminals, with face toward
  picture bottom. Approximate working dimensions are 2.00 m front-to-back,
  1.54 m lateral spread and 1.55 m total height. These are a common plausible
  interpretation, not a claim the illustrated projections are geometrically exact.
- Use one shared scale and one fixed rest geometry. Body is narrow relative to
  the wide planted stance. Three limb root stations lie approximately at
  Y=+0.42,-0.27,-0.72 m; these are coarse guidance read from LEFT, not exact joint
  measurements. Head is toward +Y. Builders choose their own construction.

Known contradictions retained: FRONT/REAR show wide symmetric footprints while
LEFT/RIGHT overlap pairs and disagree on bend angles and foot projection. TOP's
fore/aft span is shorter than LEFT and its anterior limb folding differs. HERO
has overlapping limb segments and ambiguous depth relationships. Torso/head
scale and neck angle also vary. Use the masters above consistently, label these
residual mismatches, and do not invent an anatomically idealized unseen design.
Never reshape geometry per camera, repaint/repair the source, independently
rescale a reference, or change the criteria to improve scores.

Budget: 1,000-32,000 triangles and one 1024 px texture the script paints. Semantic node
names should identify torso, head, face and each of the six limbs and feet for
later rigging. Separate nodes or joined skin are allowed. Avoid floating anatomy,
joint gaps, accidental fused leg bridges and hidden duplicate limbs.

All five orthographic views count equally; HERO is visual evidence only.
Numeric score = (2*overlap + zones + edges)/4. Resemblance >=0.4 is a diagnostic
gate; it is not a quality promise. No calibrated upright standing table is
asserted for this horizontal inconsistent creature. Visual priorities: six
traceable limbs; gaunt humanoid head/torso; plausible shared proportions and
stance; skin relief/colour; clean surfaces that can articulate.

Exactly five successful modeling evaluations per builder, at most fifteen
submissions including failures. Preserve failures, inspect every comparison and
record self-review honestly. Report visual choice, numeric leader and latest
candidate separately. Freeze each builder's own visual choice and SHA256 before
rigging. No construction code or generated geometry is shared across builders.
